# Zeitgeist

## Question

Is the APS citation network's global heavy-tailed degree distribution better
understood as a mixture of research communities with distinct citation regimes
and active time windows?

## Current state

Zeitgeist is an independent analytical program, not a required stage of the
Literature Workbench. The full §§1–4 LNCS paper draft is compiled and awaiting
review feedback. The data, Leiden, community-fit, NST, Time Curves, and K17-RGC
subgraph phases have all run. NST and Time Curves were excluded from the paper
after negative or uninformative results; a time-axis-aware layout remains
post-paper work.

## What we know

- [The APS 2022 graph contains 709,803 papers and 9,833,191 citation edges](../../archive/wiki-legacy/zeitgeist/codebase-map.md).
- Leiden found 446 communities; the 25 major communities cover 99.8% of papers,
  have fitted exponents from 2.099 to 3.268, and are temporally localized.
- Full-corpus NST time coordinates had the wrong-sign year correlation
  (Spearman ρ = −0.668), and corpus-level Time Curves were uninformative.
