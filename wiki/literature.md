# Literature

Only works that materially changed the program's methodology or evaluation
belong here. Detailed system comparisons remain in
[`archive/wiki-legacy/reference-systems/deep-dives.md`](../archive/wiki-legacy/reference-systems/deep-dives.md).

| Key | Work | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| CohenHope2026MUSE | [MUSE: A Full-Text Cross-Domain Knowledge Base of Scientific Problems, Solutions, and Rationales](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10974) | Establishes source-grounded problem–solution–rationale extraction as a practical representation for the workbench. |
| Wu2026InternAtlas | [Intern-Atlas: A Methodological Evolution Graph as Research Infrastructure for AI Scientists](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.28158) | Motivates method-level evolution edges and bottleneck-driven lineage reconstruction. |
| LiOuyang2026GRASP | [Graph-Reasoning Aided Survey Planning for High-Fidelity Related Work Generation](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1815/) | Supports planning reviews around inter-paper relations instead of flat paper summaries. |
| SelectReadWrite2025 | [Select, Read, and Write](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.366/) | Supports selective full-text reading and shared evidence state between reading and writing. |
| DeBruin2023SYNERGY | [SYNERGY systematic-review dataset](https://github.com/asreview/synergy-dataset) | Provides an external citation-bearing corpus on which the co-citation result replicated. |
