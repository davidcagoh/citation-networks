# LitReview v2 Prompt: Automated Literature Review & Citation Graph Traversal

**Topic Description:**
The intersection of automated literature review generation, citation graph traversal methodologies, and stopping criteria for abstract screening. We are specifically interested in systems that use Large Language Models (LLMs) or active learning to automate systematic reviews, the structural properties of citation networks (such as scale-free distributions and hub suppression) that affect traversal, and statistical or heuristic stopping rules used to bound the screening process.

**Inclusion Criteria:**
- Papers proposing or evaluating automated systems for literature reviews, systematic reviews, or scientific surveys (especially LLM-based or RAG-based systems).
- Methodological papers on citation snowballing, graph traversal for information retrieval, or handling heavy-tailed/scale-free degree distributions in citation networks.
- Papers defining or evaluating stopping criteria, yield thresholds, or continuous active learning heuristics for abstract screening.
- Must be peer-reviewed publications, preprints from major repositories (arXiv, medRxiv), or canonical foundational papers.

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Papers that merely *conduct* a systematic review using standard manual methods without proposing or evaluating automation tools.
- General graph theory papers that do not apply to information retrieval, citation networks, or document clustering.
- General LLM benchmark papers that do not specifically address scientific literature synthesis or RAG over academic corpora.

**Seed Papers (to bootstrap the Escape Hatch):**
1. *Guidelines for snowballing in systematic literature studies and a replication in software engineering* (Wohlin, 2014) - [Citation snowballing methodology]
2. *Statistical stopping criteria for automated screening in systematic reviews* (Callaghan & Müller-Hansen, 2020) - [Stopping rules / Screen yield]
3. *Emergence of scaling in random networks* (Barabási & Albert, 1999) - [Scale-free networks / Hubs]
4. *Automated literature research and review-generation method based on large language models* (Wu et al., 2025) - [LLM review generation]
5. *The SAFE procedure: a practical stopping heuristic for active learning-based screening in systematic reviews and meta-analyses* (Boetje & van de Schoot, 2024) - [Active learning stopping heuristics]
